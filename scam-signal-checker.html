How it works

The checker scans the text for signals that commonly appear in scams: urgency, requests for credentials or codes, unusual payment methods, prize or too-good-to-be-true offers, impersonation of support or authority, suspicious links and requests to move off-platform. Each signal has a weight, and the weights are added into a risk score (0 to 100). It is a simple rule-based demo, not a trained model, so it can miss scams and flag harmless messages. A human reviewer should always make the final decision.
